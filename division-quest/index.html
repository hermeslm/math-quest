🎬 Watch and learn together

  1. Watch first. Sit with your child and play the Visual Learning Animation. It shows the idea of the lesson step by step.
  2. Pause and ask. When the animation asks a question, pause it. Let your child answer before you continue.
  3. Retell it. At the end, ask your child to explain the method in their own words, or to show it with a drawing.
  4. Still stuck? Watch the Another Look Video. It explains the same idea in a different way.
  5. Practice. Work through the example below together, try the home activity, then start a Division Quest test.
